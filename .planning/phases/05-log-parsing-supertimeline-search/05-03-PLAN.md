Phase-4 hash-match infra and the established orchestrator/store-owner-ordering patterns. Search is
streaming (no index — SEARCH-03/v2 is out of scope) and PERF-01-conscious (never slurp a volume).

parser slice (05-02), so the two run in parallel.
