<objective>
Close gap **G-2 (MAJOR, failed UAT test 9)**: the LOG-03/D-44 shell-history tamperability
finding and the D-45 log-completeness/rotation finding are COMPUTED by the parsers/discover
but DROPPED before the report — `analyze --logs --search` produced a report with **0**
occurrences of "tamper" in report.json/report.html.

Thread both findings from where they are computed, through `run_logs`, into the case
(persisted), and render them in `assemble_report_body`'s `log_findings` section as an explicit
neutral disclosure sub-block (both report.json and report.html).

editable by the subject (D-44, observed-fact only) and that a rotated log set may be incomplete
(D-45). Without it the report silently presents shell-history command order as if it were
chronological truth — a forensic honesty-disclosure failure.
